Automatic export pickup

Press Export in Roca, and the cards appear here for review. Choose the folder where your browser saves Roca’s CSV files.

Save settings, then export a new CSV to this folder. Existing files are left alone. New exports open when the workspace is free; others wait in a queue. Leave blank to turn pickup off.

Automatic price research

Every new card joins the research queue. Intake values and retail label prices are separate and always require approval.

The public TCGCSV feed supplies daily TCGplayer market references. TCGplayer individual sold records and PriceCharting data access are not connected. Free-feed suggestions are enabled by default for exact English, Near Mint matches and are clearly marked as market references. Slabs and played cards need matching evidence.
Defaults: 90% intake value and 100% retail. Cash/store-credit buying percentages then apply to intake value. No paid feed is activated.
